## Steps
**Step 1** : Click on the {img:../img/tw_icon_side.png} (Single Invoice side icon) given on the right side of the Billing Activity screen.
![](../img/twc_01.png)
**Step 2** : Upon clicking {img:../img/tw_icon_side.png}, User will be shown the Single Invoice screen.
**Step 3** : Enter the query fields with desirable input for querying data and click on {img:../img/tw_btn_search.png}.
![](../img/twc_02.png)
**Step 4** : Upon clicking {img:../img/tw_btn_search.png}, the BL details and charges with matching information are returned.
**Step 5** : Select the required Settlement Type and Charge Type.
**Step 6** : Either select all charges available by clicking on {img:../img/tw_cb_all.png}(select all checkbox) or select individual charges by clicking on {img:../img/tw_cb_single.png}(single select checkbox).
**Step 7** : Click on {img:../img/tw_btn_credit_invoice.png}.
![](../img/twc_03.png)
**Step 8** : Upon clicking {img:../img/tw_btn_credit_invoice.png}, the user is shown Credit Invoice pop up screen.
**Step 9** : Enter the details as given below and click on {img:../img/tw_btn_check_list.png}.
![](../img/twc_04.png)
If successfully validated, a success message is shown as given below.
**Step 10** : Click on {img:../img/tw_btn_process.png}.
![](../img/twc_05.png)
If successfully processed, an alert with the System Generated Invoice Number is shown as given below.
![](../img/twc_06.png)
