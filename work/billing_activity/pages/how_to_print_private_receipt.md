## Steps
**Step 1** : Click on the {img:../img/icon_government_receipt_query.png} (Government Receipt Query side icon) given on the right side of the Billing Activity screen.
![](../img/grq_print_pr_01.png)
**Step 2** : Upon clicking {img:../img/icon_government_receipt_query.png}, User will be shown the Government Receipt Query screen.
**Step 3** : Enter the From/To Date and click on {img:../img/btn_search.png}.
![](../img/grq_print_pr_02.png)
**Step 4** : Upon clicking {img:../img/btn_search.png}, receipts with matching information are returned.
**Step 5** : Click on the menu icon {img:../img/icon_row_menu.png} of the required receipt and select "**Reprint Private Receipt**" from the menu.
![](../img/grq_print_pr_03.png)
**Step 6** : Upon clicking "**Reprint Private Receipt**", User will be shown the Reprint Private Receipt pop up screen.
**Step 7** : Select the Charge Type and Print Charge and click on {img:../img/btn_submit_reason.png}.
![](../img/grq_print_pr_04.png)
If successfully printed, a success message is shown as given below.
![](../img/grq_print_pr_05.png)
The Private Receipt pdf is displayed as given below.
![](../img/grq_print_pr_06.png)
