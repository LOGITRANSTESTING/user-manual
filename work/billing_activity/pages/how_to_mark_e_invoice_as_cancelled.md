## Steps
**Step 1** : Click on the {img:../img/icon_e_invoice_query.png} (E-Invoice Query side icon) given on the right side of the Billing Activity screen.
![](../img/ein_cancel_01.png)
**Step 2** : Upon clicking {img:../img/icon_e_invoice_query.png}, User will be shown the E-Invoice Query screen.
**Step 3** : Enter the BL Number, select the required Invoice Status and click on {img:../img/btn_search.png}.
![](../img/ein_cancel_02.png)
**Step 4** : Upon clicking {img:../img/btn_search.png}, E-Invoices with matching information are returned.
**Step 5** : Click on {img:../img/icon_row_menu.png}(menu icon) of the required invoice and select "**Cancel Marking**".
![](../img/ein_cancel_03.png)
**Step 6** : Upon clicking "**Cancel Marking**", the user is shown Cancel Marking pop up screen.
**Step 7** : Enter the details as given below and click on {img:../img/btn_submit.png}.
![](../img/ein_cancel_04.png)
The status of the invoice is changed to Cancel as given below.
![](../img/ein_cancel_05.png)
