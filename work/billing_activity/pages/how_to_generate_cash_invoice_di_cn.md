## Steps
**Step 1** : Click on the {img:../img/di_icon_side.png} (Direct Invoice side icon) given on the right side of the Billing Activity screen.
![](../img/di_01.png)
**Step 2** : Upon clicking {img:../img/di_icon_side.png}, User will be shown the Direct Invoice screen.
**Step 3** : Select the required Settlement Type.
**Step 4** : Add charges by clicking on {img:../img/di_btn_add.png} under Tariff Charges / Local Charges and enter the charge details.
**Step 5** : Click on {img:../img/di_btn_calculate.png}.
![](../img/di_cash_01.png)
**Step 6** : Upon clicking {img:../img/di_btn_calculate.png}, the invoice amounts are calculated and the Cash Invoice and Credit Invoice buttons are enabled.
**Step 7** : Click on {img:../img/di_btn_cash_invoice.png}.
![](../img/di_cash_02.png)
**Step 8** : Upon clicking {img:../img/di_btn_cash_invoice.png}, the user is shown Cash Invoice pop up screen.
**Step 9** : Enter the details as given below and click on {img:../img/di_btn_process.png}.
![](../img/di_cash_03.png)
**Step 10** : Upon clicking {img:../img/di_btn_process.png}, the user is shown Tax Invoice pop up screen.
**Step 11** : Enter the details as given below and click on {img:../img/di_btn_ok.png}.
![](../img/di_cash_04.png)
If successfully processed, an alert with the System Generated Receipt Voucher Number is shown as given below.
![](../img/di_cash_05.png)
