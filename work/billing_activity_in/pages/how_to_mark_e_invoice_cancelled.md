## Steps
**Step 1** : Click on the {img:../img/icon_e_invoice_query.png} (E-Invoice Query side icon) given on the right side of the Billing Activity screen.
![](../img/in_ein_cx_01.png)
**Step 2** : Upon clicking {img:../img/icon_e_invoice_query.png}, User will be shown the E-Invoice Query screen.
**Step 3** : Enter the BL Number and click on {img:../img/btn_search.png}.
![](../img/in_ein_cx_02.png)
**Step 4** : Upon clicking {img:../img/btn_search.png}, e-invoices with matching information are returned.
**Step 5** : Click on the menu icon {img:../img/icon_row_menu.png} of the required e-invoice and select "**Cancel Marking**" from the menu.
![](../img/in_ein_cx_03.png)
**Step 6** : Upon clicking "**Cancel Marking**", User will be shown the Cancel Marking pop up screen.
**Step 7** : Select the Cancel Reason, enter the Text Print and click on {img:../img/btn_submit_ein.png}.
![](../img/in_ein_cx_04.png)
If successfully marked, a success message is shown as given below and the status of the e-invoice is updated to Cancel.
![](../img/in_ein_cx_05.png)
