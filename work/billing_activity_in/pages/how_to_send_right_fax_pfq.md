## Steps
**Step 1** : Enter the query fields with desirable input for querying data and click on {img:../img/btn_search.png}.
![](../img/in_pfq_rightfax_01.png)
**Step 2** : Upon clicking {img:../img/btn_search.png}, proforma invoices with matching information are returned along with their details.
**Step 3** : Click on {img:../img/icon_right_fax.png} (Right Fax icon) of the required proforma invoice.
![](../img/in_pfq_rightfax_02.png)
If successfully generated, a success message is shown as given below.
![](../img/in_pfq_rightfax_03.png)
